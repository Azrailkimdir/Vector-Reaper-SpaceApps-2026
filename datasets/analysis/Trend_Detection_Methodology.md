# Trend Detection Methodology

## Objective

Detect environmental changes by comparing recent observations with historical averages.

---

## Indicators

1. Temperature
2. NDVI
3. Drought Index

---

## Formula

Trend Change (%) =

(Current Value - Historical Average)
/ Historical Average

× 100

---

## Classification

0-5%
Stable

5-15%
Moderate Change

15%+
Significant Change

---

## Output

- Trend Direction
- Risk Score
- AI Summary
