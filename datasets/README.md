# Datasets

This directory contains the environmental datasets used by EarthPulse AI for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

---

## Purpose

The objective of this folder is to store, organize, and document all datasets used to detect environmental trends, generate insights, and support data-driven decision making.

These datasets provide the foundation for EarthPulse AI's trend detection, risk assessment, and visualization systems.

---

## Planned Data Sources

### NASA EarthData

Used for:

- Climate Monitoring
- Surface Temperature Analysis
- Environmental Change Detection

### MODIS

Used for:

- Vegetation Monitoring
- NDVI Analysis
- Land Surface Temperature
- Wildfire Detection

### Landsat

Used for:

- Long-Term Environmental Change
- Urban Expansion Analysis
- Water Surface Monitoring
- Vegetation Change Detection

---

## Environmental Indicators

EarthPulse AI may analyze:

- Surface Temperature
- NDVI (Vegetation Index)
- Drought Indicators
- Land Cover Change
- Environmental Risk Factors

Raw Data
