# AI Insights Generator

## Purpose

Generate human-readable environmental summaries from Earth observation data.

---

## Example Input

Temperature Change: +2.4°C

NDVI Change: -8%

Risk Level: Moderate

---

## Example Output

EarthPulse AI Environmental Summary

Surface temperature increased by 2.4°C compared to historical averages.

Vegetation levels decreased by 8%.

Current environmental risk level: Moderate.

These indicators may suggest increasing environmental stress and should be monitored over time.
