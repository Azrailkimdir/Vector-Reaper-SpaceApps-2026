# EarthPulse AI User Story

## Scenario

A student, teacher, researcher, or citizen scientist wants to understand how environmental conditions are changing in a specific region.

Instead of searching through multiple complex NASA datasets, the user opens EarthPulse AI and selects a location.

The platform automatically analyzes environmental data and presents trends in a simple, visual format.

---

## Example User Journey

### Step 1

User selects a location.

Example:

Ankara, Turkey

---

### Step 2

EarthPulse AI retrieves environmental indicators.

Examples:

- Vegetation Index (NDVI)
- Surface Temperature
- Drought Indicators
- Historical Environmental Trends

---

### Step 3

The system compares recent observations against historical averages.

---

### Step 4

The dashboard displays:

- Temperature Trend
- Vegetation Trend
- Environmental Risk Score
- Interactive Map

---

### Step 5

AI generates a natural-language explanation.

Example:

"Vegetation levels have decreased by 8% compared to the historical average. If current trends continue, drought risk may increase during the upcoming season."

---

## User Benefits

- Easier access to NASA Earth observation data
- Better understanding of environmental change
- Support for education and citizen science
- More informed environmental awareness

---

## Mission

Transform complex environmental datasets into understandable insights for everyone.
