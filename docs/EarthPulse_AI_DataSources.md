# EarthPulse AI Data Sources

## Goal

Identify NASA datasets capable of detecting environmental trends.

---

## Candidate Data Sources

### MODIS

Purpose:
- Vegetation Monitoring
- Wildfire Detection
- Land Surface Temperature

Potential Indicators:
- NDVI
- Burned Areas
- Thermal Anomalies

---

### Landsat

Purpose:
- Long-Term Environmental Change

Potential Indicators:
- Urban Expansion
- Vegetation Loss
- Water Changes

---

### NASA EarthData

Purpose:
- Climate and Earth Observation

Potential Indicators:
- Temperature Trends
- Soil Moisture
- Atmospheric Conditions

---

## Selected Use Case

Initial prototype will focus on:

- Vegetation Change Detection
- Temperature Trend Analysis
- Environmental Risk Visualization
