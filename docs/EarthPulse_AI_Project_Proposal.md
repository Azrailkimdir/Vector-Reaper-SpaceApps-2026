# Problem Statement

NASA and its partner agencies collect massive amounts of Earth observation data every day. While this information is valuable, much of it remains difficult for students, educators, and the general public to understand or interpret.

Environmental changes such as drought, vegetation loss, abnormal temperature increases, and climate-related anomalies often develop gradually and may go unnoticed until they begin to create significant impacts.

The challenge is not a lack of data.

The challenge is recognizing meaningful trends before they become larger problems.

---

# Proposed Solution

EarthPulse AI is an environmental trend detection platform that combines NASA Earth observation datasets, data visualization, and artificial intelligence.

The platform will help users:

- Detect environmental trends
- Monitor changes over time
- Explore Earth observation datasets
- Identify potential risk areas
- Understand environmental data through AI-generated explanations

By transforming complex datasets into accessible insights, EarthPulse AI aims to make environmental intelligence more understandable and actionable.

## AI-Powered Insights

The platform will generate easy-to-understand explanations such as:

"Vegetation levels in this region have decreased by 9% compared to the five-year average."

"This area shows an increasing drought trend based on recent observations."

## Environmental Risk Scoring

EarthPulse AI will provide a simple risk score based on detected environmental trends.

Possible categories:

- Low Risk
- Moderate Risk
- High Risk
