# Environmental Risk Model

## Objective

Create a simple and understandable environmental risk scoring system using NASA Earth observation data.

---

## Indicators

EarthPulse AI evaluates:

- Surface Temperature
- Vegetation Index (NDVI)
- Drought Indicators

---

## Method

Each indicator receives a score from:

0 → Normal

50 → Moderate Concern

100 → High Concern

---

## Environmental Risk Score

Risk Score =

(Temperature Score × 0.4)
+
(NDVI Score × 0.4)
+
(Drought Score × 0.2)

---

## Risk Categories

0 – 30
Low Risk

31 – 60
Moderate Risk

61 – 100
High Risk

---

## Example

Temperature Score = 70

NDVI Score = 50

Drought Score = 40

Risk Score =

(70 × 0.4)
+
(50 × 0.4)
+
(40 × 0.2)

= 56

Result:

Moderate Risk

---

## Goal

Provide users with an intuitive understanding of environmental change and emerging risks.
