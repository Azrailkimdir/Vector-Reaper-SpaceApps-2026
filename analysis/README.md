# Analysis

This directory contains the analytical framework used by EarthPulse AI to detect, evaluate, and explain environmental trends.

---

## Purpose

The objective of this folder is to document the methodologies, calculations, findings, and environmental insights generated throughout the project.

EarthPulse AI aims to transform NASA Earth observation data into understandable environmental intelligence through trend detection and risk assessment.

---

## Analysis Areas

### Environmental Trend Detection

Comparing recent observations against historical averages to identify significant environmental changes.

Examples:

- Temperature Trends
- Vegetation Change (NDVI)
- Surface Change Detection
- Drought Indicators

---

### Risk Assessment

Developing environmental risk scores based on observed trends and indicators.

Risk Categories:

- Low Risk
- Moderate Risk
- High Risk

---

### Statistical Analysis

Methods used:

- Trend Analysis
- Historical Comparisons
- Change Detection
- Environmental Scoring

---

## Documents

This folder will contain:

- Trend Detection Methodology
- Risk Assessment Models
- Environmental Analysis Reports
- Statistical Findings

---

## Mission

Detect meaningful environmental trends before they become larger problems.
