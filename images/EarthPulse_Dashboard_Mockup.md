# EarthPulse AI Dashboard Mockup

## Purpose

The EarthPulse AI Dashboard is the primary interface through which users explore environmental trends using Earth observation data.

The dashboard transforms complex environmental datasets into understandable insights through visualizations, environmental indicators, and AI-generated explanations.

## Core Dashboard Elements

### Environmental Health Score

Provides an overall indication of environmental conditions within the selected region.

### Trend Indicators

Displays:

- Temperature Change
- Vegetation Change
- Drought Risk
- Environmental Change Rate

### Interactive Map

Visual representation of environmental indicators and risk zones.

### AI Environmental Summary

Generates human-readable explanations of environmental changes and detected trends.

### User Actions

Users can:

- Change Location
- Compare Regions
- Download Reports
- Explore Historical Data
